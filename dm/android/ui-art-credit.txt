CONTROLLER BUTTON ICON ART — ATTRIBUTION
========================================

The controller button icons in app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi (ctl_ns_*, ctl_xb_*, ctl_ps_*: the face buttons and the d-pad)
come from Zacksly's button icon packs, taken as bundled in PlayFieldPortal (core-ui, same files).

  Author:   Zacksly
  Website:  https://zacksly.itch.io
  Support:  https://www.patreon.com/zacksly
  License:  Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
            http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Packs in use (all "Buttons Solid / White / 128w"):

  - PS5 Button Icons and Controls          -> ctl_ps_*   (DualSense)
  - Xbox Series Button Icons and Controls  -> ctl_xb_*
  - Switch 2 Button Icons and Controls     -> ctl_ns_*

Modification statement (required by the license):
  The artwork is UNMODIFIED — the bundled PNGs are byte-identical to the pack originals.
  Only the file names were changed, to Android resource names, and just the four face buttons and the
  whole d-pad of each pack are included.

BUTTON PROMPT FONTS (dm/tools/make_ext_font_icons.py)
------------------------------------------------------

dm/tools/make_ext_font_icons.py makes a stand-in for the Switch's button font from the same three packs, for games that draw their
prompts with the system font. No such font is shipped in this app: Fire Emblem Fortune's Weave draws its prompts from its own font file.

Modification statement (required by the license), for fonts made with that tool:
  The pictures are MODIFIED: each PNG is traced into an outline (the edge of its alpha channel), scaled so a face button fills the
  font's line, and centred. Shapes are not redrawn. The eight stick direction arrows in such a font are not from the packs.
